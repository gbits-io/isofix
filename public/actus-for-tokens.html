Why UMP and not a money-market type?

ACTUS does not currently define a money-market-fund-share contract as a distinct type. The closest fit for daily-accrual, callable, no-fixed-maturity instruments like BUIDL, USYC, BENJI, USTB and OUSG is UMP (Undefined Maturity Profile) — the same type Swiss savings accounts collapse into. A proper MMF type would add NAV state, scheduled distribution events and a defined redemption mechanism, all of which are computable from these tokens' contract state and would be needed for a clean semt.002 mapping.

USDY is modeled as PAM (Principal At Maturity) since it's structured as a note rather than a fund share, even though it functions as a yield-bearing stablecoin. The pool-based credit products (Centrifuge, Maple, Goldfinch) sit under LAM (Loan At Maturity) as compositions of underlying loans.
