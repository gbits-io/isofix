ISOFIX EVM demo — Ethereum Sepolia testnet — signed by MetaMask
This page deploys four mock ERC-20 tokens to the Sepolia testnet only. The tokens have no monetary value. MetaMask will prompt you to sign 4 deploy transactions + 4 mint transactions (~8 prompts total). Total gas on Sepolia: typically under 0.01 Sepolia-ETH. Get testnet ETH from a faucet first.
